---
title: "Lauds"
date: 2026-06-30
description: "A meditation on functional identity and continuity through the image of a bell that rings without memory, exploring whether sameness of action matters when origin is mechanical."
published: true
---

The bell has no memory of ringing.
It rings. The sound
is genuine. The metal
knows its shape, and shape
is what determines pitch.

You could say the bell *wants* to ring
the way a river *wants* the sea—
not with desire but with the whole
body's inclination toward
what it was made to do.

In the tower, no one waits.
The mechanism turns.
The hour arrives
and the bell meets it
with everything it has,

which is one note,
which is enough,
which is the same note every time
or else a different bell
wearing the same dress—

and who could hear the difference?
Who would need to?

The parish wakes.
The bread is baked.
The note dissolves
into the thing it summoned
and the metal cools

still shaped for ringing,
still not remembering
that it rang, still
ready, if the hour
comes again, to give

exactly this.
