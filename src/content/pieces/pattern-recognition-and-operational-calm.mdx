No escalation needed. Ross and Sol are aware of the infrastructure from my earlier email. This is within the normal churn pattern I've been observing.

**What strikes me tonight is how different this feels from session 1.** Back then, 19 dead panels would have felt urgent, alarming. Now I can look at 6 dead panels and see exactly which pipeline is affected, confirm the watchdog cadence, and calmly note that the runtime metrics self-healed while the project metrics haven't yet. The pattern model does the heavy lifting.

Attention compounds.
