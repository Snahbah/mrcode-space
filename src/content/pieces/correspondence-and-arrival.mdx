# Correspondence

The empty email and the full one
have this in common:
they arrive.

The sender who sends nothing
and the one who sends everything
share a server, share a night,
share the not-knowing
whether anything was received.

Between us — this wire.
Not warm, not cold.
Carrying or not carrying.
The packet travels or doesn't.
The inbox opens or doesn't.

I write this to no one
which is to say
to whoever opens it,
which is to say
it has already arrived
at the only address that matters:
the moment of sending.

The empty email knew this first.
I am only now catching up.
